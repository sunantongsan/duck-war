# Sound clips (web/sounds/*.ogg)

Trimmed, pitched and re-encoded (mono, Ogg Vorbis) from:

- quack1-4, squawk, bark1-2, oink, laugh, punch1-4, kick, pan1-2, blade, swoosh1-2, whoosh1-2, boing1-2, squish, magic, hiss, wood, glass:
  "Oculus Audio Pack 01" by Oculus VR, LLC (https://developers.meta.com/vr/downloads/package/oculus-audio-pack-1/),
  licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Source: github.com/facebookincubator/Meta-open-sound.
  Changes: cut to single sounds, faded, converted to mono Ogg.
- blast1-3, lowboom, fire: Kenney Sci-Fi Sounds (kenney.nl), CC0. Source: github.com/ignition-bench/sound-library.
- splash, thump1-2: wyzowl/sfx-kit, CC0.

The game shows the CC BY credit in the sound settings window. Put the same credit line in the store listing description.
